Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MyQuickBill GST billing and CRM suite.

How does payment through the dynamic UPI QR code work?
MyQuickBill encodes your configured Merchant UPI VPA (e.g. yourname@icici) along with the exact final invoice bill into a standard UPI QR code. The buyer simply opens GPay, PhonePe, Paytm or BHIM, scans the screen, and the exact amount is pre-filled.
Can I print bills on standard thermal POS printers?
Yes. The software supports printing formatted 3-inch (80mm) thermal receipts as well as full A4 tax invoices for tax records and B2B customers.
Do I need to buy a POS machine or barcode hardware?
No. MyQuickBill runs right in your web browser on your existing laptop, desktop, tablet, or phone. You don't need to rent or buy proprietary hardware.
Is MyQuickBill cloud-based and secure?
Yes. MyQuickBill is hosted on secure Tier-2 cloud infrastructure with 256-bit SSL encryption, automatic daily backups, and 24/7 access from any device.
